Quiet Steps, Lasting Foundations

Dear Friends,

Some updates arrive without a dramatic story, yet they matter because they become the foundation for everything that follows. We are grateful to share three such developments—and to praise the Lord for His faithfulness in each one.

Two Websites, One Vision

Our online discipleship training is taking shape as a growing pathway for anyone, anywhere, to go deeper in the Word and learn to walk in step with the Spirit. It remains a work in progress, and that is why your experience and feedback are valuable.

At the same time, the Museum of the Bible Korea has taken its first public step as a virtual museum. Long before a physical building can rise in Seoul, we are building a digital front door—a place where visitors can discover what the Bible has contributed to Korea and consider what it can still contribute across Asia.

A Legal and Organizational Milestone

CrossCulture Community is now formally established in Korea as an unincorporated nonprofit association and has received its Korean organizational tax number. This step does not make headlines, but it provides a real foundation for membership, partnerships, local giving, and the organizational development still ahead.

After more than four decades of ministry in Korea, we are thankful to see relationships, vision, and legal structure beginning to come together for the next season.

How You Can Partner With Us

Pray that the vision will reach the right hearts; that God will provide for the discipleship platform and the museum; and that CrossCulture Community will grow through committed members, partners, and friends.

Participate by visiting and sharing the websites, offering thoughtful feedback, introducing us to people who may connect with this work, and becoming part of the community.

An Invitation to See the Bible Anew

Ultimately, this is about more than websites or organizational milestones. It is an invitation to recognize together that the Bible matters even more than many of us currently realize—not only in the abstract, but for our lives, families, communities, and, as Korea's history demonstrates, the character of nations.

Wherever you are reading from, we invite you to take part in that awakening. Thank you for your prayers, encouragement, generosity, and faithful friendship.
